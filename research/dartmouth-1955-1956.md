# Dartmouth proposal 1955, workshop 1956

## Dates and people

The archived proposal PDF (Wayback copy of `jmc.stanford.edu/articles/dartmouth/dartmouth.pdf`, hash in `SNAPSHOT-HASHES.md`) contains the date August 31, 1955.

Proposal authors named in the t472 source note, which matches the standard public account: John McCarthy, Marvin Minsky, Nathaniel Rochester, Claude Shannon.

The workshop was held at Dartmouth in the summer of 1956. McCarthy’s historical page was among the sources snapshotted in t472: the Stanford formal-site history page served from `www-formal.stanford.edu` (hash `1bb0f35b972cb0bd4970bbba2b112730c78d8bed427cd8498fe89845586f70c7` for that HTML snapshot; the HTML is not republished).

Warren Weaver is part of the Shannon–Weaver communication-model story and of earlier machine-translation advocacy. He is not treated as a co-author of the 1955 AI proposal. Attendance at the 1956 workshop was not established from the files read for this release.

Ray Solomonoff attended and circulated inductive-inference material. Trenchard More attended and worked on deductive decisions. Those two are not proposal authors.
