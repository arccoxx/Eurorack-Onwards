# Residual Trick — citations and sources (Stage 1)

- Colin Cherry, "Some Experiments on the Recognition of Speech, with One and
  with Two Ears," J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 25, 975 (1953). Cocktail party effect.
- Pierre Comon, "Independent Component Analysis, a New Concept?," Signal
  Processing 36, 287 (1994).
- Bell & Sejnowski, "An Information-Maximization Approach to Blind Separation
  and Blind Deconvolution," Neural Computation 7, 1129 (1995).
- Hyvärinen, "Fast and Robust Fixed-Point Algorithms for Independent
  Component Analysis," IEEE Trans. Neural Netw. 10, 626 (1999).
- Lee & Seung, "Learning the Parts of Objects by Non-Negative Matrix
  Factorization," Nature 401, 788 (1999).
- Wiener, *Cybernetics* (1948); Ashby, *An Introduction to Cybernetics* (1956).
  Error signal = residual = absorbed variety.
- Kuramoto, *Chemical Oscillations, Waves, and Turbulence* (1984).
- Stuart & Landau, amplitude equations near Hopf bifurcation (the normal form
  behind parametric-oscillator reductions).
- Leleu, Yamamoto, McMahon, et al., "Combinatorial Optimization Using the
  Lagrange Primal-Dual Dynamics of Parametric Oscillator Networks," Phys.
  Rev. Applied 21, 044042 (2024). Shows coupled-oscillator Ising solvers
  exactly implement primal-dual Lagrange optimization.
- "Analyzing Parametric Oscillator Ising Machines through the Kuramoto Lens,"
  Phys. Rev. Applied (2026) / arXiv:2510.24416. Phase-sum coupling term;
  amplitude heterogeneity degrades solution quality.
- "On Computational Capabilities of Ising Machines Based on Nonlinear
  Oscillators," arXiv:2105.07591. Polynomial scaling, SDP-relaxation
  equivalence.
- GPU-accelerated simulated oscillator Ising/Potts machines, arXiv:2505.22631.
  11295× speedup, 99% accuracy at scale.
- Doepfer A-196 PLL manual: https://doepfer.de/a100_man/A196_man.pdf
- CD74HC4046A PLL datasheet (TI).
- Sound + Voltage, "What the h_ll is a PLL?" (YouTube, Feb 2026) — deep dive
  on the A-196: internal VCO, phase comparator, loop filter; FM-broadcast
  patch using Radio Music + A-196.
- HRTF: Duda & Martens, "Range Dependence of the Response of a Spherical
  Head Model"; Woodworth's ITD formula; SOFA conventions.
- Jay Hack, Active Sound Control Lab (continuous ring + moving quiet bubble).
- Canon standing-wave detection patent; Yamaha cabin standing-wave attenuator
  patent; UW seven-bot mic swarm (mute-zone carving).
- 2026 arXiv: privacy-aware audio occupancy sensing (raw sound → occupied/not,
  no speech storage).
